Questions people ask after they smell the crawl space

Is a vapor barrier the same as encapsulation?

No. A liner on the dirt can leave vents open. A closed crawl usually seals vents, covers walls, and adds humidity control. Ask which one is on the quote.

Should crawl space vents stay open in Charlotte?

Summer air here is wet. Open vents can add moisture under the floor. Ask whether the plan keeps vents open or closes them.

Do I always need a dehumidifier?

Not on every liner job. A sealed space often needs one. Ask if the unit, outlet, and maintenance are in the written total.

What if there is standing water?

Covering a puddle is not waterproofing. Ask about drainage and a sump before anyone tapes plastic.

What mil liner should I ask for?

Thickness claims vary. Ask the mil number, how seams are taped, and how far the liner runs up the wall. Then take photos when they finish.

Does Mecklenburg County require a permit?

A simple liner may not. Drainage, wood repair, and new electrical for a sump or dehumidifier often do. Ask the shop and confirm with county code enforcement.

How do I avoid a scare-quote?

Get the moisture source in writing. Compare liner-only versus closed-crawl line items. Walk the finished space before you sign. Charlotte phone numbers are listed so you can ask more than one shop the same questions.
