Musty air and soft floors. Ask if they are selling a sheet of plastic or a closed crawl.

Charlotte humidity plus clay soil makes cheap liners and $15,000 “encapsulation” quotes look the same on the phone. They are not. Use this list before you book.

  • Ask what the moisture source is — groundwater, grading, or humid air through the vents — before anyone sells a liner.
  • Ask whether this is a vapor barrier only (vents often stay open) or a closed crawl (vents sealed, walls covered, dehumidifier).
  • Ask if drainage and a sump are in the written price or extra.
  • Ask the liner mil, how seams are taped, and whether you get photos of the finished space.
  • Ask what the warranty covers, if it transfers on sale, and whether drainage or electrical needs a Mecklenburg permit.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website and run through this list.
